Decision: Cache raw answers, score afterwards
Date: 2026-11-05
Status: accepted
Enforced by: decision.cache-key, scoring.pure
Evidence: docs/architecture.md:187, src/decision/decide.ts:177-183

### Context

Model calls are slow and paid, and weights and thresholds are tuned often.

### Decision

The cache key is the provider, model, endpoint, questions and content. The value is the raw answers, and scoring.ts runs after every lookup.

### Alternatives

Caching scores instead, rejected because every change of a weight would then ask the model again.
